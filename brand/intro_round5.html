My read
  1. Logo: H2, three hills. It has the strongest silhouette in the 48 px line-up and still reads as hills at 16 px. The story works in one look: two partisan ranges, with the voters in the middle standing in front in purple. No election brand uses a landscape, and the hills are really opinion curves, so data people will get the second meaning.
  2. One risk to check: mountains are common in outdoor and film logos (Patagonia, Paramount). The bell-curve shapes and the blue, red and purple keep it reading as data, so keep the curves smooth and never add snow caps or a sun.
  3. If you'd rather keep dots: D3.6. The two electorates with the purple middle growing reads first as an overlap, not a Venn diagram. The pies stay strong but are the most chart-like of the three directions.
